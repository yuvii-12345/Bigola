03 — Deep Space

Facts That Break Your Brain

🕳️

Black Holes Eat Time

Near a black hole, time itself slows down. An hour for you could be a year for someone watching from far away. Gravity doesn't just bend space — it bends reality.

💎

Diamond Planets Exist

Scientists believe there are planets made largely of diamond — twice Earth's size, with oceans of liquid carbon. Somewhere out there, it rains diamonds.

🌊

Space Is Not Silent

Neutron stars — the crushed cores of dead suns — spin 600 times per second. Their magnetic fields sweep the cosmos like lighthouse beams, pulsing in radio waves.

⏳

Your Atoms Are Star Stuff

Every carbon atom in your body was forged inside a dying star. You are, quite literally, the universe experiencing itself. Bigola just made it interactive.

🌌

More Galaxies Than Stars

There are an estimated 2 trillion galaxies — more than there are stars in the Milky Way. The observable universe is almost entirely empty space.

⚡

Lightning on Jupiter

Jupiter's storms produce lightning 1,000× more powerful than Earth's. The Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than our entire planet, raging for 300+ years.
